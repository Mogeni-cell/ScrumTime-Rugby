About ScrumTime Rugby

ScrumTime Rugby is a platform for sharing rugby fixtures and match results with fans in an easy and accessible way. It allows teams, organizers, and supporters to stay updated on upcoming matches, match venues, kickoff times, and final scores. Our goal is to make following rugby matches simple, organized, and enjoyable for everyone.
